# Forbidden Plot Shortcuts

## Non-negotiable prohibitions

1. **No canon invention**: do not invent facts, identities, relationships, motives, history, rules, powers, conditions, thresholds, costs, exceptions, or endings.
2. **No plot-by-mechanism**: do not turn each worldbuilding rule into a scene or sequence. Mechanisms constrain and affect character action; they do not replace it.
3. **No motivation patching**: do not invent a motive solely to make a character perform a convenient action.
4. **No knowledge teleportation**: do not let characters use information they have not acquired.
5. **No capability inflation**: do not grant abilities, resources, access, or authority that the project has not established.
6. **No forced relationship**: do not infer family, romance, friendship, mentorship, rivalry, or allegiance from proximity, resemblance, or convenience.
7. **No arbitrary coincidence rescue**: do not solve a blocked plot by introducing an unsupported coincidence.
8. **No compulsory antagonist**: do not add a villain, pursuer, mastermind, conspiracy, or hostile organization unless the project supports it.
9. **No compulsory violence**: do not equate conflict with combat, threat, or physical confrontation; obey the project's genre limits.
10. **No fixed exchange rate**: do not impose a cost formula, sacrifice, punishment, or consequence that the rules do not establish.
11. **No premature reveal**: do not disclose identities, relationships, mechanisms, or answers earlier than the project permits.
12. **No false foreshadowing**: do not label an incidental detail as a planned clue or claim it proves more than it does.
13. **No ending by convention**: do not force a final battle, restoration, victory, defeat, or total explanation.
14. **No template takeover**: do not impose three-act structure, hero's journey, fixed chapter counts, standard arcs, or generic genre beats over project instructions.
15. **No inference laundering**: do not write a proposed event as if it were a necessary deduction.
16. **No silent canonization**: do not insert brainstormed content into authoritative files without author confirmation.
17. **No unauthorized editing**: do not modify project files unless implementation is clearly requested.
18. **No false verification**: do not claim to have read, checked, tested, or changed material that was not actually accessed or modified.

## When a plot path gets stuck

Do not force a dramatic event. Instead identify which element is missing:
- character motive;
- available information;
- feasible action;
- opposing response;
- causal trigger;
- established rule;
- timeline space;
- author decision.

Then show what can still be reasoned about without that missing element.
